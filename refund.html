Refund Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are handled for our digital software products, presets, educational materials, and related services.

1. Digital Product Notice

Our products are digital software tools and educational materials delivered electronically. Once access, files, presets, or license information have been delivered, the product is considered accessed.

2. Refund Window

Refund requests may be reviewed within 7 days of purchase. Approval depends on whether the product has been accessed, downloaded, delivered, or used.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds may not be granted when the digital product has already been delivered, downloaded, accessed, or used; when the request is based on trading results; or when the issue is related to user configuration, market conditions, broker conditions, or unsupported platform usage.

4. No Performance-Based Refunds

Because financial markets involve risk and results vary, refunds are not provided based on expected profits, trading performance, backtest differences, or market outcomes.

5. Technical Issues

If you experience a technical issue, contact support first. We will make reasonable efforts to help with installation, setup, and product access.

6. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same product more than once, contact us with your payment details so we can review the duplicate transaction.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a chargeback. We are willing to review legitimate issues and help resolve access or technical problems.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund review, email us at support@yourdomain.com with your name, purchase email, order ID, and the reason for the request.
